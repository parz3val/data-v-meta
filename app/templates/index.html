grade a live English Wikipedia article with the models from the dissertation

Each model reads what the collector fetches now: revision properties, wikitext, contributor history and ninety days of pageviews. Metadata models see thirty-three counts and never the prose; the text encoder reads the first 128 and last 382 tokens of the article. Nothing typed here is stored. Grades are the WikiProject scale from Stub to FA; a model's grade is a prompt to look, not a verdict.
